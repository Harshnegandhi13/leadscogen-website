About Leadscogen

A simple way to start a financial conversation

Leadscogen is a financial-services facilitation and lead-generation platform. We are a sole proprietorship, not a bank, NBFC or regulated lender.

What we do

We help individuals and businesses across India share their loan, insurance or mutual-fund requirement in one simple form. We then coordinate with banks, NBFCs, housing finance companies and other appropriate financial partners through DSA, channel-partner or referral arrangements, as applicable, to help your enquiry reach the right place.

What we are not

  • We are not a bank or NBFC.
  • We do not lend money ourselves.
  • We do not sanction loans or determine interest rates.
  • We do not guarantee loan approval, refinancing or any specific outcome.
  • We are not an insurance broker or a SEBI-registered investment adviser, unless explicitly stated with verified registration details.

Our approach to compliance

We take data protection and regulatory requirements seriously, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, applicable RBI guidance for lending service providers and DSAs, TRAI's commercial-communication requirements, and applicable insurance/mutual-fund distribution norms. Where a particular activity requires a specific licence, registration or authorization, we do not claim to hold it unless it genuinely exists.
