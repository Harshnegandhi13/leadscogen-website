What we ask consent for

Before submitting your enquiry, you are asked to separately confirm:

  • Enquiry/transactional consent (required): to be contacted by Leadscogen regarding your enquiry, and for your information to be shared with relevant financial partners where necessary to respond to your request.
  • Marketing consent (optional): to receive occasional updates about new loan, insurance or mutual-fund solutions. This is kept separate and is never pre-ticked.

TRAI / commercial communication

We aim to contact you in line with applicable TRAI regulations on commercial communication. If you have registered your number under the National Do Not Disturb (DND) registry, please note that by providing explicit consent through this form, you are permitting us to contact you regarding your specific enquiry.

Withdrawing consent

You may withdraw either or both forms of consent at any time by writing to grievance@leadscogen.in. Withdrawing marketing consent will not affect our ability to respond to an enquiry you have already submitted and not withdrawn.

Children

This website and its services are intended for individuals who are at least 18 years old and legally capable of entering into a financial enquiry or contract in India.
