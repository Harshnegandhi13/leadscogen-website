A quick primer

  • SIP (Systematic Investment Plan): investing a fixed amount at regular intervals.
  • Lump sum: investing a one-time amount.
  • Equity funds: invest primarily in company shares; returns are market-linked and not guaranteed.
  • Debt funds: invest primarily in fixed-income instruments.
  • Hybrid funds: a mix of equity and debt exposure.
  • Goal-based investing: aligning investment choices to a specific future goal and timeframe.

Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. This page is general educational content only and does not constitute investment advice or a recommendation of any specific fund.
