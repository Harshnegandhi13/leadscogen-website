Overview

A personal loan is an unsecured loan that can generally be used for a range of personal requirements. Leadscogen helps you share your requirement once; we then coordinate with appropriate financial partners in our network who may be able to assist, based on their own eligibility and underwriting criteria.

Who may consider this

  • Salaried individuals with a regular monthly income
  • Self-employed professionals and business owners
  • Individuals consolidating existing high-cost debt
  • Anyone with a time-bound personal expense

Common use cases

  • Wedding or family function expenses
  • Medical treatment or emergency expenses
  • Travel or education-related costs
  • Home renovation or furnishing
  • Debt consolidation

Documents generally required

Exact documentation varies by lender and applicant profile. This is a general indicative list only.

  • Identity proof (PAN, Aadhaar or equivalent)
  • Address proof
  • Recent income proof (salary slips or ITR, as applicable)
  • Bank statements for the last few months
  • Passport-size photograph

Factors lenders may consider

  • Monthly income and repayment capacity
  • Existing obligations and credit history
  • Employment type and stability
  • Requested loan amount and tenure

Eligibility varies by lender and customer profile. Leadscogen does not determine eligibility, interest rate or approval — these are decided solely by the respective lender after their own assessment.

Frequently asked questions

Is Leadscogen a lender?

No. Leadscogen is a facilitation platform. We help share your enquiry with appropriate financial partners; the lender takes the final decision.

How soon will I be contacted?

We aim to contact you during your selected callback slot, or at the earliest available time if you choose that option.

Will submitting this form affect my credit score?

Sharing your enquiry with us does not itself affect your credit score. Any credit check is performed only by a lender, with your consent, as part of their own process.
