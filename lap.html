Overview

A loan against property (LAP) lets you raise funds by pledging an owned residential or commercial property as collateral. Leadscogen helps route your enquiry to appropriate financial partners in our network.

Who may consider this

  • Property owners seeking a large-ticket loan
  • Business owners needing funds against an asset
  • Individuals consolidating multiple loans
  • Applicants exploring refinancing of an existing LAP

Common use cases

  • Business expansion funding
  • Large personal expenses (education, medical, etc.)
  • Debt consolidation
  • Refinancing an existing loan against property

Documents generally required

Exact documentation varies by lender and applicant profile. This is a general indicative list only.

  • Identity and address proof
  • Property ownership and title documents
  • Income proof
  • Bank statements
  • Existing loan statement, if refinancing

Factors lenders may consider

  • Property value, type and location
  • Income and repayment capacity
  • Existing liabilities on the property
  • Clear and marketable title, as assessed by the lender

Eligibility varies by lender and customer profile. Leadscogen does not determine eligibility, interest rate or approval — these are decided solely by the respective lender after their own assessment.

Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum amount I can borrow?

This depends on the property value and the lender's loan-to-value policy, determined solely by the lender.

Can I use the property while repaying?

Typically yes — usage terms are set by the lender and specified in the loan agreement.

Is legal verification handled by Leadscogen?

No. Legal and technical verification of the property is carried out by the respective lender.
