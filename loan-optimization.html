What loan optimization means here

Loan optimization is not a product in itself — it is a way of reviewing your existing borrowing and new requirements together, so we can route your enquiry to the most relevant part of our financial network. We help you explore available options; the respective lender makes the final decision on any refinancing, balance transfer, top-up or restructuring.

Please note: we do not promise EMI reduction, cost savings, a lower interest rate, approval or refinancing of any kind. Outcomes depend entirely on the respective lender's assessment of your profile.

How the process works

  • 1. Existing loan / new requirement You tell us what you currently have, or what you now need.
  • 2. Understand requirement We note down the specifics of your situation.
  • 3. Review available options We look at which categories in our network may be relevant.
  • 4. Coordinate with partners Your enquiry is shared with appropriate financial partners.
  • 5. You evaluate the offer If a partner responds, you review the offer on its own merits.
  • 6. Lender's final decision Approval, rate and terms are decided solely by the lender.

Common journeys we support

  • Existing home loan → explore a balance transfer enquiry
  • Existing loan against property → explore a balance transfer enquiry
  • Existing business loan → explore a refinancing enquiry
  • Existing loan → explore a top-up enquiry
  • Multiple liabilities → explore restructuring or consolidation possibilities
  • A new requirement altogether → explore suitable lending options from the start

What to keep handy

Having a few details ready helps us route your enquiry more accurately — your current lender name, approximate outstanding amount, current EMI, and what you're hoping to explore (lower EMI, extra funds, fewer loans to track, etc.). None of this is mandatory to get started.

Frequently asked questions

Will my EMI definitely reduce?

We cannot promise this. Any change in EMI, rate or tenure depends entirely on the evaluating lender's assessment of your profile and their current offerings.

Is balance transfer guaranteed once I submit this form?

No. Submitting an enquiry starts a conversation with our network — it does not guarantee a balance transfer, refinancing or any specific outcome.

Does Leadscogen charge me for this service?

Leadscogen's role is facilitation. Any charges related to the actual loan, processing or refinancing are set and disclosed by the respective lender.
