Overview

A home loan helps finance the purchase, construction or renovation of residential property. Leadscogen helps you share your requirement with appropriate housing-finance partners in our network.

Who may consider this

  • First-time home buyers
  • Individuals constructing on an owned plot
  • Those renovating or extending an existing home
  • Applicants exploring a home loan balance transfer

Common use cases

  • Purchase of a ready or under-construction home
  • Self-construction on an owned plot
  • Home renovation or extension
  • Balance transfer of an existing home loan

Documents generally required

Exact documentation varies by lender and applicant profile. This is a general indicative list only.

  • Identity and address proof
  • Income proof (salary slips / ITR, as applicable)
  • Property-related documents
  • Bank statements
  • Photographs and application form

Factors lenders may consider

  • Income and repayment capacity
  • Property value and location
  • Credit history
  • Loan-to-value requirements of the lender

Eligibility varies by lender and customer profile. Leadscogen does not determine eligibility, interest rate or approval — these are decided solely by the respective lender after their own assessment.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply if I already have a home loan elsewhere?

Yes — you can explore a balance transfer or top-up under our loan optimization section.

Is property valuation done by Leadscogen?

No. Valuation and legal verification are carried out by the respective lender or their appointed valuers.

What loan tenure is available?

Tenure varies by lender and applicant profile and is finalised by the lender at the time of approval.
