Overview

A business loan can help fund working capital, expansion, equipment purchase or other business needs. Leadscogen captures your requirement and coordinates with financial partners in our network who may be able to assist, subject to their own evaluation.

Who may consider this

  • Small and medium business owners
  • Self-employed professionals with a registered business
  • Businesses looking to expand or manage cash flow
  • Businesses seeking equipment or inventory funding

Common use cases

  • Working capital for day-to-day operations
  • Business expansion or new outlet setup
  • Equipment or machinery purchase
  • Inventory or raw-material funding
  • Short-term cash flow gaps

Documents generally required

Exact documentation varies by lender and applicant profile. This is a general indicative list only.

  • Business registration / incorporation proof
  • PAN of business and proprietor/partners/directors
  • GST registration, where applicable
  • Bank statements (business account)
  • Financial statements or ITR, as applicable

Factors lenders may consider

  • Business vintage and turnover
  • Cash flow and repayment capacity
  • Existing business liabilities
  • Industry type and business model

Eligibility varies by lender and customer profile. Leadscogen does not determine eligibility, interest rate or approval — these are decided solely by the respective lender after their own assessment.

Frequently asked questions

Can a new business apply?

Lender requirements on business vintage vary. Share your details and we will coordinate with partners whose criteria may fit your profile.

Do I need collateral?

Business loans can be secured or unsecured depending on the lender and amount. This is decided by the lender, not by Leadscogen.

What if my business has existing loans?

You can mention existing liabilities in the form — this helps us route your enquiry appropriately.
