Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE] · Draft for legal/compliance review before production launch — this is not legal advice.

This privacy policy is a draft prepared as a starting point. It must be reviewed and finalised by a qualified Indian lawyer/compliance professional, and updated with your actual registered business details, before the site goes live.

1. Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry on this website, we collect information you choose to share, such as your name, mobile number, email address, city, state, the financial product you're enquiring about, approximate amount, employment type, approximate income/turnover, details of any existing loan, and your preferred callback date/time.

We do not ask for, and you should never share with us, your OTP, PIN, CVV, ATM PIN, internet banking password, UPI PIN, card credentials or any other banking login credentials.

2. Why we collect it

  • To understand your requirement and respond to your enquiry.
  • To contact you at your preferred callback time.
  • To route your enquiry to appropriate financial partners in our network, where relevant to your request.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. How it may be shared

With your consent, your enquiry details may be shared with relevant banks, NBFCs, housing finance companies, insurance or mutual-fund partners in our network, solely for the purpose of responding to your enquiry. We do not sell your personal information to third parties for unrelated marketing purposes.

4. Retention

We retain your information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described above and to meet applicable legal, regulatory or record-keeping requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymised in line with our internal retention practices.

5. Data security

We aim to apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including secure data handling practices and restricted access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we encourage you to share only the information necessary for your enquiry.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of your personal information, and to withdraw consent for further contact or data sharing. To exercise these rights, write to us using the contact details below.

7. Consent withdrawal

You may withdraw your consent to be contacted, or to further processing of your information, at any time by writing to grievance@leadscogen.in. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

8. Grievance / contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns, please contact: grievance@leadscogen.in
